# One Place for Grok Bot

One Place finds homes for sale and for long-term rent in 31 European countries. It collects
listings from the public web and shows each home one time, even when many listings show it.

With this plugin, a Bot can:

- Search listings by place, price, bedrooms or rooms, living area, features ("sea view",
  "garden") and what the photos show ("exposed wooden beams").
- Read one listing: the description, the facts, the price history and the link to one-place.com.
- Save a search in your One Place account. One Place then emails you new matching listings, so
  the Bot does not have to search again on a schedule.
- Contact the seller of a listing or ask for a visit. The Bot shows you the message first and sends
  it only when you say yes. The seller sees your name and the email of your One Place account, and
  replies to you by email.

## Connect

The first time a Bot uses One Place, it asks you to connect your One Place account. You sign in
on one-place.com (with Google or an email link) and approve the connection. You can disconnect at
any time in your account settings on one-place.com, in the Connected apps section.

The connection lets the Bot search listings, create saved searches in your account, and send
contact requests to sellers that you approve. It does not let the Bot read your messages or change
your account settings.

## Try it

1. "Find a 2-bedroom apartment for sale in Lisbon under 500,000 euros with a terrace."
2. "Tell me more about the second one."
3. "Watch this search for me and tell me when new ones come up."
4. "Ask the seller of the first one if I can visit on Saturday."

## Limits

- One Place does not list holiday or short-term rentals.
- One Place cannot book visits or make offers. It sends your request, and the seller replies to
  you by email.
- One contact request per listing every 30 days, and at most 20 a day.
- Prices are the asking prices in the listings. They are not valuations.
