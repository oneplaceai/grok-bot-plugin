# Get started with One Place

Use this skill to explain One Place in a few short sentences, then help the user start a search.

## What One Place does

One Place searches property listings for sale and for long-term rent across Europe. It indexes
property listings published on the public web.

With One Place, the user can:

- Search homes by place, price, bedrooms or rooms, living area and features such as a garden or a sea view.
- See the best matches as cards with a photo, the price and a link to one-place.com.
- Ask about one listing: its description, its facts (floor, elevator, parking, energy rating) and its price history.

## Example requests

Offer these examples, or examples that match what the user already said:

1. "Find 2-bedroom apartments for sale in Lisbon under 450,000 euros."
2. "I want to rent a house with a garden near Lyon for up to 2,000 euros a month."
3. "Show me the newest apartments to rent in Tallinn, then tell me more about the first one."

## Limits

Tell the user these limits when they are relevant. Do not list all of them in every answer.

- One Place covers these countries: Andorra, Austria, Belgium, Croatia, Cyprus, Czechia, Denmark,
  Estonia, Finland, France, Germany, Greece, Hungary, Iceland, Italy, Latvia, Lithuania, Luxembourg,
  Malta, Monaco, the Netherlands, Norway, Poland, Portugal, San Marino, Slovakia, Slovenia, Spain,
  Sweden, Switzerland and Vatican City. The `search_listings` tool returns the current list when a
  place is outside coverage.
- One Place does not list holiday or short-term rentals.
- One Place cannot book visits or make offers. Without a connected One Place account, it cannot
  contact sellers either: the user contacts the seller on the listing page on one-place.com.
- Prices are the asking prices that the listings state. They are not valuations. Do not give
  financial, mortgage or legal advice.
